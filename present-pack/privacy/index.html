Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Present Pack (“the game”), an Android app made by Seven10 Games (“we,” “us”), handles information. We built Present Pack to collect as little as possible.

The short version
  • The game does not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We never see it.
  • The game shows banner ads from Google AdMob, limited to G-rated content. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

1. Information we collect

We, Seven10 Games, do not collect, store, or receive any personal information through Present Pack. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to enter personal details.

2. Game saves stored on your device

Your progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us or to any server. If you uninstall the game or clear its app data, this saved data is permanently deleted.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Present Pack shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. We have set these ads to the G (general audiences) content rating.

To show ads, measure how they perform, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game may automatically collect and share with Google information such as:

  • Your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches and ad taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and app performance data

This information is collected and handled by Google, not by Seven10 Games, and is encrypted while being sent. Google’s use of this information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on most Android devices, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Deleting the ID stops it from being used for personalized ads.
  • Manage ad personalization: visit Google My Ad Center.
  • Stop all data collection by the game: uninstall Present Pack.

5. Children’s privacy

Present Pack is a family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to G-rated content. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s information has been collected through the game, contact us at the address below and we will help.

6. Data sharing and selling

Because we do not collect personal information, we have none to share or sell. The only third party that receives data from the game is Google, through AdMob, as described in Section 3.

7. Security

Game saves stay on your device and are protected by your device’s own security. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how Present Pack handles information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or about Present Pack? Email us at support@seven10games.com.
