Walk through your front door to shop, nap, sleep or swap tools in the closet. Sell your harvest at the roadside stand by the road, and check the odd jobs board next to it. You carry 4 tools and 4 kinds of seeds; store the rest in the closet or a shed. Fences and gates keep rabbits and rats out, but they chew on them. Repair chewed fences with your hands before they wear through. Walk close to a pest to shoo it.
