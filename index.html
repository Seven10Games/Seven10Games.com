Who we are

Made by Family

Seven10 Games started the way a lot of good things do: at home, with family. What began as game nights and “what if we made our own?” turned into a real studio, run by a parent and two kids.

Everyone has a say. The kids pitch ideas, draw characters, and are our toughest playtesters. If a game isn’t fun for all of us, it doesn’t ship.

Our process

How We Make Games

Every game, on screen or on the table, goes through the same four steps.

01

Dream It Up

Ideas come from everywhere: family brainstorms, car rides, and the games we already love. The best ones get written down.

02

Build a Prototype

Paper, pencils, and cardboard for board games. Quick, rough builds for video games. We get it playable fast.

03

Playtest Together

Family game night becomes test night. We play, argue, laugh, and change the rules until it feels right.

04

Polish & Share

We add the art, sounds, and finishing touches, then put it out into the world for other families to enjoy.
